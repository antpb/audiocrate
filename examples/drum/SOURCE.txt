  DSP/*                                  ->  dsp/

Unlike amp, grain, and synth this is not copied from a VST3 port, because
there is no VST3 drum. The source is the AUv3 kernel itself, so dsp/ still
carries AudioToolbox and vDSP: homecrate_drumDSPKernel.mm includes
AudioToolbox for AUParameterAddress, and IRConvolver.cpp is the vDSP
overlap-save convolver. Nothing here is compiled.

dsp/ is the specification, not a build input. src/reference/ is a
line-by-line TypeScript port of it, and the parity tests hold the ASL graph
to that port. When the AUv3 kernel changes, re-copy dsp/ and the tests will
say what moved.

Not copied: the Swift voice (playhead, SOLA stretch, sequencer, sample
record), which is sampler plumbing rather than the 026S character chain.
README.md lists what the ASL material reproduces and what it does not.
